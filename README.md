# The Payload

The Payload is a defense and space technology directory. Use it to find
companies by country and sector, see what they offer, and compare products
and services side by side.

## Find a Company

- Open **Companies** and search by company, offering, or location.
- Type a founding country or its two-letter code, then narrow your search by
  sector. Satellite sectors cover spacecraft and payloads, launch and
  logistics, connectivity and ground systems, and Earth observation.
- Use **Sort companies** to rank reported funding, employee counts, or the
  number of named investors, founders, and customers. Funding is grouped by
  currency. Unknown figures appear last.
- Open a profile for company stats, offerings, public sources, and office
  locations. Use **Compare** at the bottom to compare two companies.

## Compare Products and Services

Open **Products & Services** to search by product category or supplier.
Select two or three offerings and choose **Compare products**. You can also
make selections directly on a company profile, including multiple offerings
from the same supplier. Each comparison shows the company, category, type,
description, and available source.

Use **Add Offering** on a company profile to record a product or service and
give it a category. The offering will appear in the catalog on your device.

## Add a Company

1. Choose **Add Company**, then enter the details or paste a website or
   LinkedIn URL and choose **Infer Details**.
2. Review the suggestions and add any missing information before saving.
3. In the LinkedIn Android app, you can also share a link through the Android
   share menu and choose **The Payload** to open a draft.

## Sign In and Use the App Offline

In the Android app, choose **Sign in with LinkedIn** when the option is
available. Sign-in opens in your browser and returns you to The Payload.
You can continue browsing as a guest.

The directory and comparisons work offline. Maps, linked sources, website
imports, and sign-in use the internet. Companies and offerings you add stay
on the device where you saved them.

## Stocks & Markets

Open **Stocks & Markets** to research publicly listed defense, UAV,
counter-UAV, and cybersecurity comparables. Choose a ticker for an external
TradingView chart or open the market chart in a browser. Charts require an
internet connection and may use delayed prices; The Payload does not provide
investment advice or claim the external quote is independently verified.

The default watchlist includes selected listed peers beyond the startup
directory. Only directory profiles with explicitly verified public listings
receive stock shortcuts. ParaZero's profile links to NASDAQ:PRZO. Private
companies do not receive stock prices. Use **Add a verified listed ticker**
to save additional market symbols on this device (exchange-qualified, such
as NASDAQ:AVAV). The symbol's ownership must be checked before reliance.
